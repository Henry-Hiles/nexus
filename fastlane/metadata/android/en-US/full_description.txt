<p>Nexus is a simple and user-friendly client for Matrix, the open and decentralized chat network. It's made with Flutter, with gomuks handling the Matrix side.</p>

<p>Features include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Spaces, threads, replies and reactions</li>
<li>End-to-end encryption, with verification and recovery keys</li>
<li>Polls, location sharing, and custom emoji and sticker packs</li>
<li>Multiple accounts</li>
<li>Notifications through UnifiedPush, or Firebase Cloud Messaging</li>
</ul>
